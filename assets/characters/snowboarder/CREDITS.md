# Snowboarder model

`snowboarder.glb` was supplied by the project owner (uploaded as
`snowboarder-onirix.zip`). It includes its own animations (Idle, Turn_Left,
Turn_Right, Fall, Start_Wave, Victory, Goal, Idle_1_Victory, Idle_2_Victory).

Source: "Snowboarder Onirix" by **onirix** on Sketchfab, licence **CC Attribution
(CC BY 4.0)**: free to use, including commercially, with credit to the author
(credited in the game's Settings screen).

Used by `scripts/rider/rider_avatar.gd`. Set `Rider > Use Imported Rider` to
off to go back to the procedural rider.
